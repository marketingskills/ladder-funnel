# The LadderFunnel Concept

A LadderFunnel is a paid acquisition system designed specifically for B2B SaaS founders to replace the traditional (and often broken) free trial model.

## The Problem with Free Trials
1.  **Trial Abuse:** Founders pay API/compute costs for users on their 5th burner email who have zero intention of paying.
2.  **Vanity Metrics:** High signup numbers mask near-zero purchase intent.
3.  **Accidental Retention:** Revenue built on people who forgot to cancel is fragile and leads to chargebacks and angry reviews.
4.  **High CAC (Customer Acquisition Cost):** Running ads directly to a $99/mo SaaS is incredibly expensive. Most visitors bounce, and payback periods extend to 6+ months, burning runway.
5.  **Cold Outbound:** SDRs waste time chasing people who aren't problem-aware.

## The LadderFunnel Solution
Instead of giving the software away for free, the founder sells a low-ticket ($7) information product that teaches the *manual method* of solving the problem their software automates.

### How it Works (The Math):
1.  **The $7 Intro Product:** Filters for intent. Anyone who pays $7 is a verified buyer, not a freeloader.
2.  **The $27 Order Bump:** A template or shortcut added at checkout. With a 40% take rate, the Average Order Value (AOV) rises to ~$15-$20.
3.  **Self-Funding Ads:** That $15-$20 AOV liquidates the cost to acquire the customer via Facebook Ads. Customer acquisition becomes effectively free on Day 1.
4.  **The SaaS Upsell:** Immediately after purchase, the customer is pitched the software ("You bought the playbook, now get the tool that automates it"). ~30% upgrade immediately. The other 70% enter an email ascension sequence as highly-qualified, warm leads.

**The Result:** Free acquisition, zero freeloaders, and a pipeline of warm buyers for the core SaaS.
