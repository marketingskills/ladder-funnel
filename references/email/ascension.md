# LadderFunnel Email Ascension Sequence

This sequence triggers *after* a customer buys the $7 Intro Product but declines the SaaS Upsell on the checkout page. The goal is to ascend them into the recurring SaaS subscription over 30 days.

## The Rule of Nurture: 90% Value, 10% Pitch
You must not spam the user with SaaS pitches. They bought a $7 training—they want to consume that training. The pitch for the software must be a natural next step to the training.

## The 30-Day Sequence Structure

When drafting this sequence, provide the user with subject lines and body copy for the following key milestones:

### Days 1-3: The Consumption Phase
*   **Focus:** Getting them to log in, watch the videos, and read the $7 playbook.
*   **SaaS Pitch:** None, or an extremely subtle P.S. (e.g., "P.S. If you want to skip the manual setup, [SaaS Name] automates this entire process.")
*   **Drafting Goal:** Write emails that remind them of the big promise they bought and give them a quick win they can implement today.

### Days 4-10: The "Hard Truth" Phase
*   **Focus:** They've seen the strategy. Now remind them how hard it is to maintain manually.
*   **SaaS Pitch:** Introduce the software as the natural progression.
*   **Drafting Goal:** "You've seen the framework. You know it works. But doing it in spreadsheets is painful. Here is a link to start your subscription to [SaaS Name] where we handle it for you."

### Days 11-20: The Case Study Phase
*   **Focus:** Proof.
*   **SaaS Pitch:** Direct.
*   **Drafting Goal:** Draft an email template where the founder can plug in a customer story. "How [Client] used [SaaS Name] to save 20 hours a week executing the strategy."

### Days 21-30: The Final Push (Urgency)
*   **Focus:** An exclusive offer.
*   **SaaS Pitch:** Direct with a deadline.
*   **Drafting Goal:** Draft an email offering a special bonus (e.g., an onboarding call, a custom template, or a discount) if they upgrade to the SaaS before the 30-day window closes.
